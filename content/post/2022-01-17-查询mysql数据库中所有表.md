查询mysql数据库中所有表

### **包含视图：**

`SELECT TABLE_NAME FROM information_schema.TABLES WHERE TABLE_SCHEMA = '数据库名';`
### **不包含视图：**

`SELECT TABLE_NAME FROM information_schema.TABLES WHERE TABLE_SCHEMA = '数据库名' AND TABLE_TYPE = 'BASE TABLE';`
