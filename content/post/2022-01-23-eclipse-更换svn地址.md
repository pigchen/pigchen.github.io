重新定位仅用于服务器 IP 地址更改、通讯协议（例如：http:// 改为 https://）更改或者服务器设置更改所导致的资源库根路径更改。

### **1. 打开svn资源库面板**

工具栏 — Window — Show View — Other
[![](/images/Pasted-image-20220123144743.png)](/images/Pasted-image-20220123144743.png)
打开 SVN资源库
[![](/images/Pasted-image-20220123145047.png)](/images/Pasted-image-20220123145047.png)
### **2. 重新定位**

右键点击svn地址，选择 重新定位...
[![](/images/Pasted-image-20220123145209.png)](/images/Pasted-image-20220123145209.png)
选择项目，下一步（next）
[![](/images/Pasted-image-20220123145520.png)](/images/Pasted-image-20220123145520.png)
更换svn地址后，点击finish，稍等即可
[![](/images/Pasted-image-20220123145540.png)](/images/Pasted-image-20220123145540.png)
